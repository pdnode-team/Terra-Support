## Rank
Apple tree > Eggplant > Apples = Carrots > Cabbages

If you are unable to water the apple tree at least 8 times during its second week, then the eggplant will yield a higher profit than the apple tree.

Please do not buy Cabbages unless absolutely necessary.

## Special Notes

- Free for 10, meaning the first ten are free to get. (Usually rent crops)
- If there is no buy price, the crop cannot be purchased at the shop
- Every week after you pay your rent, you will get 20 coins (times the multiplier)
-  `*` means it only works for prep weeks. E.g 25* / 30 = Prep weeks 25, normal 30.
- `+` means this crop requires at least two or more weeks to grow.
- `<` `>` `<=` `>=` means there are certain restrictions on water requirements. (e.g <=10 means water up to 10 times)

## Apple tree vs Eggplant
Apple tree will wither in the third week no matter watered or not.

First you need 5 water to make it become a tree then the second week you can water it 0-10 times. Every time you will get an apple ($55). So maximum 10 apples. And it will wither on third week

For one Apple tree you only can water it 15 times in two weeks. (5,10)
