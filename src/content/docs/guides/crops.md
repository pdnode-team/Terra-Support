# Crops price table

Most crops will rotate every day or week.

| Name                   | Buy         | Sell | Water | Limits          |
| ---------------------- | ----------- | ---- | ----- | --------------- |
| Carrots                | Free for 10 | 25   | 1     | Only Prep Weeks |
| Apples                 | 5           | 30   | 1     | Only Prep Weeks |
| Cabbages               | 2           | 20   | 1     | None            |
| Bok Choy               | 10          | 37   | 1     | None            |
| Eggplant <b>(Best)</b> | 15          | 75   | 2     | None            |
| Strawberries           | Free for 10 | N/A  | 1     | For Rent(W1)    |
| Pineapples             | Free for 10 | N/A  | 1     | For Rent(W2)    |
| Pumpkins               | Free for 10 | N/A  | 1     | For Rent(W3)    |

\*Free for 10, meaning the first ten are free to get. (Usually rent crops)

\*If there is no buy price, the crop cannot be purchased at the shop

\*Every week after you pay your rent, you will get 20 coins
