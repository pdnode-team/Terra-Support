First of all, welcome to Terra!

You can obtain all the essential information once you have completed the Terra onboarding steps and read the [FAQ](/faq).

For convenience, we will refer to the Pdnode Terra Support Community as either PTSC or TSC.

:::tip
Pdnode Terra Support (Community) doesn't belong to [Terra](https://terra.hackclub.com) or [Hack Club](https://hackclub.com)
:::

## I need help!
You can get support in the [#terra-help](https://hackclub.enterprise.slack.com/archives/C0B7KLRAFFW) or [#terra-support-community](https://hackclub.enterprise.slack.com/archives/C0C8PUD32F8) channels on the Hack Club Slack.

## Your relationship with Hack Club
PTSC is a community-driven initiative for Terra, created by Pidan. It has no direct affiliation with Hack Club; we do not represent Hack Club or Terra.
